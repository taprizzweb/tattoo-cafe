Hawa Mahal, the Palace of Winds

Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh built Hawa Mahal in 1799 so that the royal women could watch street festivals without being seen. It is five storeys of pink sandstone shaped like Lord Krishna's crown, with 953 small jharokha windows that keep the breeze moving through it. It is the face of Jaipur.

Most visitors only see it from the street. From our studio you see it straight on and at eye level.

Good to know

Questions before your first tattoo

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

Most guests describe it as a scratching or stinging feeling. Small pieces take only a few minutes, and you can ask for a break at any time.

How long does a tattoo take?

A small minimal tattoo usually takes 30–60 minutes. Larger custom pieces may need a few hours or more than one session. We'll tell you when we quote.

Is it safe and hygienic?

Yes. For every client we use sterile, single-use needles and cartridges opened in front of you, disposable gloves, and a sanitised workstation.

I'm only in Jaipur for a day or two. Can I still get tattooed?

Absolutely. Most travellers get a small or Hawa Mahal tattoo the same day. Send us your idea on WhatsApp before you arrive and we'll have a design ready.

Can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome when an artist is free. To be sure of a slot, message us on WhatsApp in advance.

Is there an age limit?

You must be 18 or older and carry a valid photo ID.

How do I take care of my new tattoo?

Keep it clean and apply a thin layer of the recommended balm. For two weeks, avoid swimming, direct sun and scratching. You'll get a printed aftercare guide after your session.

Is there an entry fee?

Yes. A cover charge applies for entry to the café and rooftop. Message us on WhatsApp for the current amount.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, UPI and cards, including international cards.
