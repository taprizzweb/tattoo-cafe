Jaipur aaye, Hawa Mahal dekha? Toh yahan kyun nahi aaye!

Five storeys of pink sandstone, 953 windows, and one rooftop that looks straight at it.

Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh built Hawa Mahal in 1799. Most visitors see it from the street. From our studio and rooftop you see it at eye level: golden in the morning, a silhouette at sunset, glowing amber at night.
