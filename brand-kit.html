Do & don't

Do

  • Put gold on maroon, and maroon on cream
  • Show Hawa Mahal in hero images and posts
  • Leave space around the logo, at least the height of "The"
  • Use real photos of the café, tattoos and guests

Don't

  • Stretch, recolour or add shadows to the logo
  • Put gold text on cream: it's hard to read
  • Use blue, teal or neon colours
  • Use stock photos of other cafés
